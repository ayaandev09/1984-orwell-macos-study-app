# 👁️ Minitrue Oceania // Complete App Walkthrough & Guide

Welcome to **Minitrue Oceania**, the native macOS study dashboard built for International Baccalaureate (IB) Diploma Language & Literature students and Orwellian scholars.

---

## 🧭 Navigation & Section Breakdown

### 1. 🎛️ Panoptic Command Center (Bento Hero Overview)
The launchpad displays your live textual progress and surveillance dossier:
- **Oceania Codex Mastery Ring**: Tracks completion across all 24 chapters and analytical beats.
- **Surveillance Threat Telemetry**: Live indicators tracking telescreen state, Thought Police threat levels, and chapter reading progress.
- **Part & Chapter Sidebar**: Clean navigation structured around Part One (Airstrip One), Part Two (The Brotherhood), and Part Three (Ministry of Love).
- **Global Jelly Spotlight (`⌘K`)**: Fuzzy search across Oceania axioms, Newspeak vocabulary, symbols (coral paperweight, church bells), and critical characters.

### 2. 🌊 Continuous Flow Surveillance Stream (`Stream` View)
- Unfiltered, chronological analysis of every chapter with margin notes detailing Orwell's political commentary.
- Deep historical subtext linking Oceania's bureaucratic apparatus to real-world 20th-century totalitarian regimes (Stalinism, WWII censorship, Spanish Civil War).

### 3. 🧩 Totalitarian Ideological Matrix (`Matrix` View)
- Cross-sectional analysis of **5 Core Orwellian Motifs**:
  1. *Surveillance & Panopticism (Big Brother is Watching You)*
  2. *Doublethink & Epistemic Control (2 + 2 = 5)*
  3. *Newspeak & Linguistic Determinism (Limits of language)*
  4. *Physical Rebellion vs. Mental Capitulation*
  5. *Social Stratification (Inner Party, Outer Party, Proles)*
- Character trajectory mapping tracking Winston, Julia, O'Brien, Syme, and Parsons.

### 4. ⚡ Thoughtcrime Active Recall (`Revision` View)
- Spaced repetition flashcards testing recall of critical Party slogans, Goldstein’s theory of war as permanent consumption, and Room 101 psychological breaking points.
- Prompt cards specifically calibrated for IB English Language & Literature Paper 1 and Paper 2 comparative prompts.

### 5. 📚 Newspeak Lexicon & Translator (`Glossary` View)
- Complete directory of Newspeak vocabulary across Vocabulary A, B, and C.
- Interactive Newspeak Translator simulator demonstrating how totalitarian language limits freedom of thought.

## ⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts & Power Features
- `⌘ + K`: Open Global Jelly Spotlight Search
- `⌘ + B`: Toggle Navigation Sidebar for wide monitor view
- `⌘ + I`: Open Right Inspector Drawer (Panoptic telemetry & exam traps)
- `⌘ + E`: Launch Timed Exam Sprint (5m, 15m, 45m essay planning drill)
- `Space`: Flip active recall card in Revision Mode
- `⌘ + 1`: Switch to Continuous Flow Mode
- `⌘ + 2`: Switch to Active Recall Cram Mode
